# Accessibility Statement for Lumina Capital

**Lumina Capital** is committed to providing an accessible, inclusive, and user-friendly quantitative research experience for all developers, researchers, and traders, regardless of ability or assistive technology.

---

## 🌐 Supported Environments & Standards

We strive to align our interactive studio interface and documentation with the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA** standards:

- **Bilingual Interface**: Seamless switching between English and Japanese across all tabs, reports, logs, and interactive widgets.
- **High-Contrast Dark Aesthetic**: Engineered with high-contrast text ratios (exceeding 4.5:1 for standard text) against dark obsidian background palettes (`#0a0e17`) to reduce eye strain and assist users with visual sensitivities.
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Standard browser and Streamlit keyboard shortcuts are fully supported for tab navigation, button toggles, and form inputs.
- **Screen Reader Compatibility**: Clean semantic HTML structure, labeled form inputs, and descriptive markdown headings.
- **Responsive Viewport**: Fluid layout adaptable to standard laptop screens, 4K multi-monitor setups, and mobile devices without horizontal clipping.

---

## ⚠️ Known Limitations & Ongoing Enhancements

While we strive for broad accessibility, certain cutting-edge features have inherent visual dependencies:
- **3D WebGL Causal Knowledge Graph**: The interactive 3D force-directed graph relies on WebGL hardware rendering. For screen reader and keyboard-only users, all underlying relational data, risk propagation paths, and supply-chain linkages are also rendered in accessible tabular and text formats.

---
